# How a lesson works

1. The **lesson panel** explains a step and what to build.
2. You **type the code** yourself in the editor — the files are real files in your project.
3. Press **Check** (`Ctrl+Alt+Enter` / `Cmd+Alt+Enter`). C++ Studio compiles and runs your code and tells you
   exactly what's right and what isn't — including what compiler errors mean.
4. Stuck? **Hint** reveals one clue at a time. **Reset step** puts the files back the way the step started.

Lessons get less guided as you go: from "type this with me" to "here are the requirements and some hidden tests".
